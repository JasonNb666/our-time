# Our Time

## Play

Choose **Start Game** to continue or begin your first journey. **New Journey**
lets you start again or restore a previous journey; your current save is
automatically archived before it is replaced.

## Controls

- **WASD / arrow keys:** move.
- **E:** interact or leave an activity.
- **F:** companion interactions.
- **I:** inventory.
- **M:** map.
- **Esc / Quit:** save and exit the game.
- **W / S / arrow keys:** select menu entries; **E / Enter / Space:** confirm.
- Buttons also support the mouse.

Explore your home and garden, grow crops and flowers, care for the Cloudling,
cook and eat together, study, change outfits, and spend time with your companion.
The food wheel, memory album, real-world tree growth, anniversaries, clocks,
stargazing, and saved journeys are included.

## Saves

Progress saves automatically and when you quit.
